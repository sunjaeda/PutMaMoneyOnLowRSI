Attractiveness: Strong (70–100) Fair (50–69) Weak (30–49) Poor (0–29)
How the attractiveness score works
  • RSI (40%) — more oversold scores higher (RSI 20 → full marks, 40 → none).
  • Trend vs 200-day MA (30%) — a dip within an uptrend (price above the 200-DMA) mean-reverts more reliably, so it scores higher.
  • Market cap (20%) — larger caps are less likely to be value traps (log-scaled).
  • Liquidity / volume (10%) — higher average volume is easier to trade (log-scaled).

A weighted blend, 0–100. Missing inputs are dropped and the rest re-weighted. This is a heuristic to rank a watchlist — not a buy signal or price target.
